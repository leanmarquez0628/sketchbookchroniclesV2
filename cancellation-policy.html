Cancellation Policy

Plans change. This page explains when you can cancel a poster order or a commission, and what happens to your payment.

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Shipping PolicyRefund PolicyCancellation PolicyTerms of ServicePrivacy Policy

1. Poster orders

Sending the order form is a request to buy. It becomes a confirmed order only after I email you to confirm availability and shipping.

  • Not yet paid: you can cancel at any time by emailing me your order reference.
  • Paid but not yet shipped: you can cancel and I will refund your payment in full.
  • Already shipped: the order can no longer be cancelled. If something is wrong with it, see the Refund Policy.

2. Unpaid orders

After I confirm your order, I send my payment details. If I do not receive payment within 7 days, I may cancel the order and release the item to other customers. You are welcome to order again at any time.

3. Commissions

  • Before I start the sketch: full refund of any payment made.
  • After I start, before you approve the sketch: I will refund your payment minus a fair charge for the work already done. I will tell you the exact amount before I refund anything.
  • After you approve the sketch: the final artwork is already underway, so the commission can no longer be cancelled.

4. If I need to cancel

Sometimes I have to cancel an order, for example when an item is out of stock, a price or description was listed wrongly, I cannot ship to your area, or a payment looks suspicious. If that happens, I will tell you by email and refund any payment you made in full.

5. How to cancel

Email me at leanmarquez0628@yahoo.com with your order reference and, if you like, a short reason. A cancellation counts only once I confirm it in writing, so please wait for my reply before assuming it is done.

6. Refunds

Approved refunds are paid to the account you paid from, as described in the Refund Policy. Questions? Email leanmarquez0628@yahoo.com or call or text +63 921 358 4345. Please include your order reference (it looks like SBC-261126-ABC123) so I can find your order quickly.
