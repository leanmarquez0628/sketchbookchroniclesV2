Shipping Policy

How long each kind of order takes to make and ship, what happens once it is on its way, and how international orders work.

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Shipping PolicyRefund PolicyCancellation PolicyTerms of ServicePrivacy Policy

1. Prints and framed posters

Posters are made to order. Once your payment is confirmed, please allow 3 to 5 weeks for your print or framed poster to be prepared and shipped. If there is a delay, I will let you know by email.

Shipping is not included in the poster price. I quote it by email, based on your address and the size of the parcel, before you pay.

2. Originals and commissions

Original artworks and finished commissions are processed and shipped within 1 to 2 weeks after your payment is confirmed, unless I told you otherwise. For custom commissions, the time to draw the piece is agreed with you in advance and is separate from this shipping time. Larger or framed pieces are packed more carefully and can take a little longer to arrive.

3. When your order ships

Once your parcel is handed to the courier, I will email you the tracking number and an estimated delivery date. Delivery dates are estimates, and delays can happen because of the courier, weather or holidays. Please make sure your delivery address and phone number are complete and correct, because the courier needs them to reach you. If your parcel arrives damaged or goes missing, see the Refund Policy.

4. International shipping

I can ship outside the Philippines for most destinations. Please email me first so I can quote the shipping cost for your country.

  • Shipping is quoted by email, and you pay in Philippine pesos.
  • Import duties, customs fees and taxes are set by your country, are not included in my prices, and are your responsibility.
  • Customs clearance can add time that I cannot control. A parcel held by customs is not considered lost or delayed by me.
  • If a parcel is refused, or returned because the address is wrong or the duties are unpaid, the shipping costs both ways are paid by the buyer.
