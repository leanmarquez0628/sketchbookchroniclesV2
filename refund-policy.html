Refund Policy

Every poster and drawing is made with care. If something arrives wrong, I will put it right. Here is how returns and refunds work.

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Shipping PolicyRefund PolicyCancellation PolicyTerms of ServicePrivacy Policy

1. My promise

Each poster is printed, and each commission is drawn, specially for your order. If your order arrives damaged, defective or not what you ordered, I will fix it with a replacement or a refund, at no cost to you.

2. What I cannot take back

Because every piece is prepared for you, I cannot accept returns or exchanges once an order has shipped for any of these reasons:

  • You changed your mind or no longer want the item.
  • You chose a different size or format (framed or print only) from the one you meant to order.
  • Colours look slightly different on paper from your screen. Every screen shows colour a little differently.
  • Custom commissions and digital files, which are made to your brief.

If you are unsure about a size or format, message me before you order and I will happily help you choose.

3. Damaged, defective or wrong items

Please check your parcel as soon as it arrives. If anything is wrong, email me within 3 days of delivery and include:

  • Your order reference.
  • Clear photos of the item and of the packaging, including the shipping label.
  • A short unboxing video, if you have one. It is helpful but not required.

Please keep all packaging until we have sorted it out. Once I confirm the problem, you can choose a free replacement or a full refund of the item price. If the problem was caused by my mistake or by damage in transit, I will also refund the shipping fee you paid.

4. Commissions

Before I start drawing, I send a preliminary sketch for your approval, and small adjustments can be made at that stage. After you approve the sketch, the artwork is considered to match your brief, so I cannot refund it for a change of mind. If I am unable to finish or deliver your commission, you will receive a refund of what you paid. Please see the Cancellation Policy for cancelling before the work is done.

5. How refunds are paid

Refunds are sent in Philippine pesos to the same GCash, Maya or bank account your payment came from. If that account is no longer available, tell me which account to use. I process approved refunds within 7 business days, and I will email you a transfer confirmation.

6. Lost or delayed parcels

If your parcel is delayed, send me your order reference and I will check the courier tracking with you. If the courier confirms the parcel is lost, I will either send a replacement or refund your order, whichever you prefer. See the Shipping Policy for usual timeframes. Delays caused by the courier, weather or holidays are outside my control, and delivery dates quoted by email are estimates.

7. Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy limits the rights you have under Philippine consumer protection laws.
