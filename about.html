01 — About me

Hi, I'm Miguel.

Artist, illustrator, and a lifelong doodler who turned a sketchbook habit into a creative practice.

LM.

Drawing is how I pay attention.

Hi, I'm Lean Miguel Marquez, an Illustration Artist and aspiring Digital, Painting, and Graphic Design Artist based in the Philippines. For me, drawing is an act of storytelling and structure—where what begins as a few quiet pencil lines on paper gradually comes alive as a fully realized character, a vivid face, or a compelling narrative.

My creative practice bridges the gap between traditional craftsmanship and expanding digital horizons. I enjoy creating vibrant anime & fan art with markers and colored pencils, as well as executing patient graphite portraits driven by subtle, meticulous shading. Complementing my character and illustration work, I produce expressive ink drawings, doodles, and architectural design.

As I continually evolve as a creative, I am actively expanding my skill set into digital art, modern painting techniques, and graphic design—merging my disciplined eye for layout and geometry with expressive visual media. Whether through hand-drawn textures or digital canvases, every piece is crafted with intention, precision, and a deep appreciation for visual depth.
