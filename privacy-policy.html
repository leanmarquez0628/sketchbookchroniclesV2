Policies — Your information

Privacy Policy

What information this site collects, why, who can see it and the choices you have. I follow the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

Last updated: November 26, 2026

1. Who is responsible

Lean Miguel Marquez, an independent artist trading as The Sketchbook Chronicles, in Metro Manila, Philippines, is the person who decides how your information is used (the “personal information controller”). You can reach me at leanmarquez0628@yahoo.com.

2. Information I collect

What you give me when you use the site:

  • Orders: your name, email, phone (optional), delivery address or notes, the poster, size, format and quantity you chose, the payment method and bank you selected, the total and your order reference.
  • Contact form: your name, email and the message you write.
  • Newsletter: your email address.

What the site keeps in your browser: your language and currency choices, and, if your connection fails while you send a form, a short-lived copy so the site can try again. You can clear this any time in your browser settings.

What I do not collect: this site does not take card numbers, e-wallet or bank passwords, or any payment details. Payment is a manual transfer made outside the site, through your own banking or e-wallet app. There are no customer accounts or passwords.

3. How I use your information

  • To confirm availability and shipping, arrange payment, prepare and send your order, and keep a record of it.
  • To reply to your messages and commission enquiries.
  • To send my newsletter, only if you subscribed. Every newsletter lets you opt out.
  • To protect the site and prevent spam, fraud and misuse.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

I use your information because you provided it to me, because I need it to fulfil your order, and because I am required to keep certain records. I do not sell your personal information, and I do not use it for advertising.

4. Cookies, tracking and outside requests

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. To work, it makes a few requests to outside services, and those services can see your IP address and device details like any website you visit:

  • Google Fonts to load the site’s fonts.
  • An exchange-rate service (open.er-api.com) to show estimated prices in other currencies.
  • Supabase, my database, when you load products or send a form.

5. Who can see your information

Only I can read your orders and messages. I share information only with the services needed to run the shop:

  • Supabase stores form submissions in a database located in Singapore. Access is limited to my private sign-in.
  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • My email provider receives the order emails you send me.
  • Couriers receive your name, address and phone number to deliver your parcel.
  • Your bank or e-wallet processes your payment under its own privacy policy.

I may also disclose information when the law requires it. Some providers process data outside the Philippines, and I choose providers that protect it appropriately.

6. How I protect it

The site uses HTTPS, and submissions are stored in a database where visitors can only send information and can never read what others have sent. Only my signed-in account can read orders and messages. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not send sensitive information, such as passwords or card numbers, by email or through the forms.

7. How long I keep it

I keep order records for as long as needed to complete your order, handle refunds or complaints, and meet tax and accounting requirements. Messages are kept until your enquiry is resolved and for a reasonable period afterwards. Newsletter emails are kept until you unsubscribe or ask me to delete them. After that, I delete or anonymise the information.

8. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your information is used
  • access the information I hold about you
  • correct information that is wrong or out of date
  • object to certain uses, or withdraw your consent
  • ask me to block, remove or delete your information, where the law allows
  • receive a copy of your information in a common format
  • claim compensation if you were harmed by misuse of your information

To use any of these rights, email me. I may need to confirm who you are first, and I will reply as soon as I can. If you are unhappy with my response, you can complain to the National Privacy Commission.

9. Children

This site is meant for adults. I do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent me information, email me and I will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If my practices change, I will update this page and its “last updated” date.
